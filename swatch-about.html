About Rose

Your hair isn’t difficult.

Maybe you’ve been told it is: too thick, too curly, too fine, too damaged. Maybe you’ve started apologizing for it when you sit down. You don’t have to do that here.

I’m Rose Wilkers. I do hair in Warrenton, Virginia, and it’s just me, start to finish.

Sitting in that chair takes some trust. You’re in a cape, facing a mirror, and someone you may have just met is holding the scissors. So we talk first: what you want, what went wrong last time, and how much time you really have in the morning. I’ll tell you what I’d do and what it costs before anything gets cut or mixed.

What I’m working toward is a few weeks from now, when you catch yourself in a window and like what you see, and you did it yourself that morning.

I do color, cuts for men, women and kids, curls, locs and keratin. I’ll show you how to look after it at home, because that’s where most of it happens.
